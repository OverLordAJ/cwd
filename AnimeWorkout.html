Read Before You Grind

The stuff the anime skips

Honest Note

Don't Let the Hero Arc Become an Injury Arc

Saitama's routine is a meme partly because it's brutal to sustain without a recovery plan. If you're progressing toward Tier 3, make sure sleep, protein, and rest days scale up with the volume — or the hero arc turns into an injury arc real fast.

Progression

Climb, Don't Leap

Run a full week of an arc before advancing. Jumping straight to 100-100-100 untrained is how you tweak a shoulder, not how you get strong. Even Midoriya spent ten months just cleaning a beach before the real training started.

Reality

Reps Don't Grant Superpowers

No real routine caps your strength or makes you bald — that's the fiction. But consistency, progressive overload, and recovery are exactly how real strength is built. The wrapper is anime; the discipline underneath is legit.

Safety

Check With a Human First

This is general fitness content, not medical or coaching advice. If you have injuries, health conditions, or you're new to exercise, talk to a doctor or qualified trainer before starting. Form over ego, every set.
