Read Before You Grind

The stuff the anime skips

Progression

Climb, Don't Leap

Master a full week of a rank before moving up. Jumping straight to the Saitama Protocol untrained is how you injure a shoulder, not how you get strong. Krillin trained for years before he could hang with anyone.

Reality

Reps Don't Grant Superpowers

Saitama's routine is a story device — no real routine caps your strength or makes you bald. But consistency, progressive overload, and recovery are exactly how real strength is built. The fiction is the wrapper; the discipline is real.

Recovery

Rest Is Part of Training

Muscle is built while you recover, not while you grind. Sleep, eat enough protein, and take rest days. Even Rock Lee collapsed after opening the Gates — pushing to failure daily with no recovery is a downgrade, not a flex.

Safety

Check With a Human First

This is general fitness content, not medical or coaching advice. If you have injuries, conditions, or you're brand new to exercise, talk to a doctor or a qualified trainer before starting. Form over ego, always.
